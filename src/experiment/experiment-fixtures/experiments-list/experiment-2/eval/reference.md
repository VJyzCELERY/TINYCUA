# Frontier LLM Reference Coverage

Frontier-model reports should distinguish models from providers, cite primary
model cards or benchmark documentation, and discuss capability, cost, latency,
context length, safety, and evaluation limits. A benchmark score is evidence,
not a universal ranking: quality depends on the task, prompting, tools, and
deployment constraints. Compare at least three current model families and
explain reproducibility, contamination, and independent evaluation.

The evaluator uses an 8.0 BLEU-1 threshold only as a reference-coverage signal.
Its bundled model list and evidence requirements provide a fixed comparison
corpus; claims outside that corpus do not affect scoring.
