# Neural Networks and Transformers Reference Coverage

Neural networks learn representations by optimizing a loss with gradient
descent and backpropagation. Layers transform activations through parameters
and nonlinear functions. Transformers use self-attention to relate tokens,
positional encoding to represent order, and encoder and decoder components for
understanding and generation. A study plan should progress from linear algebra
and probability to neural-network training, attention, transformer
architectures, implementation exercises, and critical evaluation.
